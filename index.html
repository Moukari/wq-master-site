Frequently asked questions

How does a retainer contract work?

You choose a tier (Essential, Standard or Site+), which sets your monthly fee and day cap. The contract runs for a 12-month minimum and then rolls with 3 months' notice. Prices are reviewed once a year in January. Days beyond the cap are billed at the day rate.

Can an external person really be our responsible welding coordinator?

Whether an external coordinator is accepted depends on your certification body, the execution class and the country. We check your certification body's expectations with you in the first call and only then agree the scope.

Do you travel? Who pays for it?

Yes. Audits, inspections and site supervision are done on site, in Europe and Asia and elsewhere on request. Travel costs, travel time and per diems are billed separately and agreed in advance. Remote work (document reviews, procedures, calls) has no travel cost.

Which languages do you work in?

English and Finnish fluently, Swedish, and basic German. Documents and reports are in English unless agreed otherwise.

How and when do we pay?

Invoices are in EUR, issued monthly in advance with 14 days net payment terms. For new clients outside the EU we ask for prepayment. Finnish VAT is added where applicable.

How do you stay independent?

We work on one side per supplier and project: we never act for both a supplier and its client on the same project, never inspect our own work, disclose existing relationships before any surveillance offer, and take no commissions from suppliers we recommend. Certification bodies certify; we prepare and run your system.

Is our information confidential?

Yes. We sign a mutual NDA before we see your documents if you wish, and we only store business contact data.

We are only just starting – is there a smaller first step?

Yes: the welding gap analysis (1,500–2,500 €, fixed price). You get a concrete report and can decide afterwards whether to continue with a retainer.

What happens as you take on more clients?

We take on a limited number of retainers so that each client gets the agreed days and fast response. As we grow, additional qualified coordinators and inspectors join; your contract, price and named point of contact stay clear.
